About

More than just code.

I'm a computer science student who got into development the practical way — by building things that saved me time, then realizing other people needed the same tools. That curiosity turned into a habit of shipping: websites, automation scripts, and scrapers that turn messy data into something usable.

I care about interfaces that feel considered rather than assembled, and about automation that quietly removes friction instead of adding complexity. On CodeWithAli, I document what I learn along the way — partly to teach, mostly to stay honest about my own process.

Right now I'm focused on getting better at the intersection of clean front-end design and practical backend automation, and building a portfolio of real, working projects rather than tutorials-followed-once.
